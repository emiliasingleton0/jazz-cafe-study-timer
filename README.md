# Jazz Café

Jazz Café is an interactive web experience designed for studying, reading, or simply background noise. Rather than opening a traditional study timer, people are welcomed into a warm jazz club where they choose a destination, discover curated records, enjoy a drink, and settle into a focused work session. Inspired by jazz cafés and listening bars around the world, the project uses its minimalist interface design to create an immersive atmosphere that feels just as enjoyable to leave open in the background as it is to actively use. Each destination introduces users to a different jazz culture through playlists, historical information, and a unique and fun atmosphere.

## Features

- Interactive jazz café experience
- Choose a destination from around the world
- Curated jazz playlists based on each location
- Historical facts and cultural information
- Cocktail recommendations
- Record crate-inspired music selection
- Focus note for study sessions
- Built-in study timer
- Vintage paper textures and typewriter animations
- Warm, minimalist visual design

## Inspiration

The biggest inspiration came from my trip to Japan in 2025, where my friends and I visited Hachigatsu no Kujira in Shibuya. The bar is known for its extensive cocktail menu inspired by music and film. If the bartender recognized the movie you requested, they would create a cocktail based on its atmosphere and themes. That experience inspired the storytelling elements throughout Jazz Café, where drinks, music, and locations all contribute to creating an experience and curating a vibe, which I love.
